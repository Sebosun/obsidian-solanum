The break scene's models (a cat asleep, a coffee mug, the bed the cat sleeps in).

Cat_sleeping.glb
Original work made for Solanum, modelled in Blender and exported as a GLB. A sleeping orange tabby
with vertex colours and one looping "Sleep" animation (breathing, ear flicks, a stirring tail).

mug.glb
Original work made for Solanum, modelled in Blender and exported as a GLB: a coffee mug with a
baked, unlit texture.

pillow.glb
Original work made for Solanum, modelled in Blender and exported as a GLB: a rectangular pet bed
with a baked, unlit texture.

The baked models (mug, pillow) carry their lighting in their textures, so the scene draws them as
they are, without tone mapping. The steam above the mug is a shader that uses no external art.
