The break scene's models (a cat asleep, a coffee mug).

Cat_sleeping.glb
Original work made for Solanum, modelled in Blender and exported as a GLB. A sleeping orange tabby
with vertex colours and one looping "Sleep" animation (breathing, ear flicks, a stirring tail).

mug.glb
Original work made for Solanum, modelled in Blender and exported as a GLB: a coffee mug with a
baked, unlit texture.

The steam above the mug is a shader and the pillow under the cat is built in code (until a
modelled one replaces it); neither uses external art.
