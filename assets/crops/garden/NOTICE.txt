Original Blender crop models made for Solanum. No external models, source art,
textures, or runtime dependencies. These are deliberately stylised, low-poly crops
with oversized produce and vibrant colours for a small presentational scene.

Tomato_1.glb through Tomato_4.glb; Tomato_produce.glb
Corn_1.glb through Corn_4.glb; Corn_produce.glb
Apple_1.glb through Apple_4.glb; Apple_produce.glb

Each family progresses through seedling, juvenile plant, fruit-setting growth,
and a mature fruit-bearing plant. Stage 4 is always the plant. The separate
_produce model contains only the picked tomato, ear of corn, or apple (with its
attached calyx, husk, or stem/leaf), never the whole plant or tree.

Rebuild with Blender 5.2:
  blender --background --python scripts/build-garden-crops.py -- tomato
  blender --background --python scripts/build-garden-crops.py -- corn
  blender --background --python scripts/build-garden-crops.py -- apple

The authoring script uses the deterministic vertex-light baker and GLB encoder in
scripts/blender_common.py and rebuilds the files byte for byte (checked with Blender 5.2.2
by tests/blender-models.test.ts, which is skipped on a machine without Blender). Interactive
runs create separate Blender lifecycle review scenes without changing other scenes.
Lighting, soft fill and contact shading are baked into vertex colours. Each GLB
contains one mesh and one unlit material, with no cameras, lights, or textures.
The app must keep these materials unlit and disable tone mapping.

All models are grounded at local Y=0 in glTF (Z=0 in Blender). Growth stages share
their family's coordinate system and are sized against stage 4, not independently.
Produce is independently sized as produce, rather than using a plant's dimensions.
