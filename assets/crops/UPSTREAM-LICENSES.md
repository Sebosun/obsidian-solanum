# Model licenses

The Quaternius files in this folder (`Carrot_1.glb` to `Carrot_4.glb`, `Corn_1.glb` to
`Corn_4.glb`, `Pumpkin_1.glb` to `Pumpkin_4.glb` and `Tomato_1.glb` to `Tomato_4.glb`) come
from one pack by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com). Nothing else here is from Quaternius:
the Coconut models and everything under `garden/` are original work made for Solanum (see
`NOTICE.txt` and `garden/NOTICE.txt`).

public domain dedication. The pack's source page names CC0 and links to that deed. CC0
needs no attribution; it is given anyway, with the provenance below.

## Provenance

The models are the GLB conversions of the pack published in
[trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase](https://github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase),
`public/glb/crops_pack/` at commit `722c39e964360beff2728a8ee4d103e2d11d457c`, copied
without modification. The showcase application is not included, and its MIT license covers
that application's code only, not these models.

That repository's notes say the packs were copied from quaternius.com in December 2022 and
matched to their pages by model names, model count and preview images, and that the
license information was last checked against quaternius.com on 2026-10-10.

## Before adding another pack

Newer Quaternius releases use the Quaternius Asset License (QAL) instead of CC0. The QAL
does not allow redistribution of the assets themselves. Check the license on a pack's
source page before adding it, and do not add a QAL pack.
