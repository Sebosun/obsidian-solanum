Subset with fontTools 4.68.0 (digits 0-9, colon and question mark, keeping the tnum feature):
  pyftsubset bricolage-grotesque-latin-700-normal.woff2 --unicodes="U+0030-003A,U+003F" \
    --flavor=woff2 --layout-features="tnum,lnum" --output-file=bricolage-grotesque-digits.woff2
The subset is bundled into the plugin as a data URL; no font service or runtime network is used.
