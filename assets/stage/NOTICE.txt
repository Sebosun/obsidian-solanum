Farm_platform.glb
Original work made for Solanum in Blender. No external models, textures, or source art.
Shared by the crop scene and monthly harvest, loaded through the vault binary adapter.

Rebuild with Blender 5.2:
  blender --background --python scripts/build-farm-platform.py
The script rebuilds the GLB byte for byte (checked with Blender 5.2.2 by
tests/blender-models.test.ts, which is skipped on a machine without Blender). It can also be run through Blender MCP
to create a separate review scene without replacing the user's existing scenes.

A chamfered rectangular farm plot: 132 triangles, one mesh, one unlit material.
Warm key lighting, a soft fill, and ray-cast contact shading are baked into vertex colours.
The matching light sources are included in the Blender review scene, not in the GLB.
There are no runtime lights, textures, cameras, or external dependencies in the GLB.

glTF coordinates: Y up; the flat planting surface is at local Y=0.
Footprint: 1.90 x 1.56 world units; earth extends down to Y=-0.245.
For a future replacement of the existing platform, place it at [0, -0.54, 0].
Keep the baked material unlit and disable tone mapping when integrating it.
Monthly harvests extend only the lower earth geometry to encode session length;
the turf rim, planting plane, baked colours and shared source GLB remain unchanged.
